How the mapping works

So you can trust what lands in the register.

  • Reported Risk Rating comes from the scanner's own severity (Nessus risk factor / CVSS, OpenVAS threat, Nmap script state). You then set Asset Classification per host, and the Actual Risk is calculated from both plus exploit availability.
  • CVEs are extracted from the finding and its description.
  • Exploit Available is read from Nessus exploitability flags where present, otherwise left Unknown for you to confirm.
  • Nmap records every open port as an Information finding (your attack-surface inventory) and lifts anything its vuln NSE scripts flag.
