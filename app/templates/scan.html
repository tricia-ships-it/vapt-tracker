What this scan does — and doesn't

  • Does: host discovery, TCP port scan, service/version fingerprint, TLS protocol & certificate checks, HTTP security-header review, risky-service flags, and version-to-CVE matching against the local dataset.
  • Doesn't: send exploit payloads, brute-force credentials, or alter targets. It makes the same benign connections any ordinary client would. Real exploitation is done manually by your team.
  • Offline: no internet is used. Only your targets are contacted, from this machine.
  • Only scan systems you are authorised to assess.
