How the mapping works

So you can trust what lands in the register.

  • Reported Risk Rating comes from the scanner's own severity. You set Asset Classification per host, and Actual Risk is calculated from both plus exploit availability.
  • CVEs are extracted from the finding and its description.
  • Exploit Available is read from Nessus exploitability flags, otherwise left Unknown to confirm.
  • Nmap records every open port as Information (attack-surface inventory) and lifts anything its vuln scripts flag.

Checks

What this scan does — and doesn't

  • Does: host discovery, port scan, service/version fingerprint, TLS & certificate checks, HTTP header review, risky-service flags, version-to-CVE matching.
  • Doesn't: send exploit payloads, brute-force credentials, or alter targets. Real exploitation is done manually by your team.
  • Offline: no internet is used. Only your targets are contacted, from this machine.
